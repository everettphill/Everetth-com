Now that we've established the power of words, I'd like share the most powerful words of all: the words of God. The more we speak the Word of God, the more our lives are pulled into an atmosphere of life in God.
Here are some scriptures that are dripping with life that would be excellent to constantly think about and speak! Some are God speaking to us, others are praising God, and all of them are powerful!


[](http://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5b85d26e5b409b32edc6ed3d/1535829343252-5V2O58RELLA3DZC3NM3M/truth.jpeg)


Philippians 4:19, **"And this same God who takes care of me will supply all your needs from his glorious riches, which have been given to us in Christ Jesus."**


2 Timothy 1:7, **"For God has not given us a spirit of fear and timidity, but of power, love, and self-discipline."**


1 Corinthians 2:16, **“Who can know the LORD’s thoughts? Who knows enough to teach him?” But we understand these things, for we have the mind of Christ."**


Proverbs 8: 17.** "I love those who love me, and those who seek me early and diligently shall find me."**


1 Peter 5:7, **"Let Him have all your worries and cares, for He is always thinking about you and watching everything that concerns you."**


John 14:27, **"I am leaving you with a gift - peace of mind and heart! And the peace I give isn't fragile like the peace the world gives. So don't be troubled or afraid."**


2 Corinthians 4: 13, **"Yet we have the same spirit of faith as he had who wrote, I have believed, and therefore have I spoken. We too believe, and therefore we speak."**


Keep confessing these verses and you will see breakthrough in your life! Feel free to post a comment with any Bible references below that you love to confess over yourselves. There are so many great verses that there's no way I could fit it into this blog!
