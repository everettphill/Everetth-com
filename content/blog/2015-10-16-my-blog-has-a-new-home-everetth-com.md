Good afternoon! I've been wanting to be able to say this for a while: My blog has its own .com home! You may be thinking, "Great for you!" Or you may be thinking, "Well, that's kind of self absorbed...". Let me explain the reason behind it: My blog has been a lot of fun to write and I've gotten fantastic feedback from people. However, the other website was naturally invisible to the internet in a lot of ways and I wanted to reach a larger audience. Also, I have a long-term plan of publishing several books, starting with *[James](/),* but had no way to market them successfully.
Here comes EverettH.com to the rescue!


With this website, I can do more and I can reach more people. God is opening up a new avenue of influence and leadership through this website and I'm stoked about it. Because it's a .com site, it is more visible on search engines. Also, when *James* is published, I can sell hard copies of the book from right here using the store functionality.


If you want to be notified by email whenever I submit a new post, put your email in the form just to the right of this post.


Please be praying that God will show me all the useful ways in which he wants me to capitalize on this new tool. Also, be praying for the Holy Spirit to give me an increased ability to convey wisdom and knowledge through my words. Above all, I desire for this site and my words to be of benefit to others.

For to one is given through the Spirit the utterance of wisdom, and to another the utterance of knowledge according to the same Spirit, to another faith by the same Spirit, to another gifts of healing by the one Spirit, to another the working of miracles, to another prophecy, to another the ability to distinguish between spirits, to another various kinds of tongues, to another the interpretation of tongues. All these are empowered by one and the same Spirit, who apportions to each one individually as he wills" (1 Corinthians 12:7-11 ESV).
