Psalm 57 is jam-packed with insights on how to live a life that glorifies God no matter what. I'm excited to share some of the ideas I've been gleaning from this song using this visual medium so you can follow along and see the connections. Let me know what strikes a chord with you or what else you've learned from this amazing chapter in the comments.








Check out these additional resources to dive deeper after watching the video:

-

Watch BibleProject's video on the structure and purpose of biblical poetry:  [https://youtu.be/q9yp1ZXbsEg](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q9yp1ZXbsEg)


-

Read 1 Samuel 24, which is the historical backdrop of Psalm 57. This link uses the Blue Letter Bible website, which is a fantastic resource: [https://www.blueletterbible.org/kjv/1sa/24/1/s_260001](https://www.blueletterbible.org/kjv/1sa/24/1/s_260001)


-

Compare this Psalm against other Psalms set to the tune of Do Not Destroy: Psalms 57, 58, 59, and 75


-

Read other Miktams (a type of Psalm): Psalms 16, 56, 57, 58, 59 and 60


-

Absorb the Psalm 57 commentary from Matthew Henry: [https://www.blueletterbible.org/Comm/mhc/Psa/Psa_057.cfm?a=535001](https://www.blueletterbible.org/Comm/mhc/Psa/Psa_057.cfm?a=535001)
