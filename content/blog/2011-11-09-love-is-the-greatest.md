1 Corinthians 13:1 "If I could speak all the languages of earth and of angels, but didn’t love others, I would only be a noisy gong or a clanging cymbal."
This verse immediately follows a chapter dedicated to talking about the gifts and talents that God has given us and how to use them. Knowing that makes this verse even more powerful. It is so important to God that we have love and exhibit it in our lives that he says, "Hold up! Don't get distracted by all these extras. They're important, but my love is the most important! I am love!"


I forget this way too many times!


Please pray that we all remember how important love is every day!
