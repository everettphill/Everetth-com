Today God put a burdon on my heart for families. I know that a lot of families are having a very difficult time now with so much brokenness, but I was also thinking about how much every single family really needs prayer. No matter where your family is at spiritually, physically or mentally, they need your prayers and it's time we pray for them! So please, set aside some time today and the rest of the week to cry out to God for his will to be done in your family. Pray for God to be at the center of it all. After all, he is the ultimate father!
Psalm 127:1 "Unless the LORD builds the house (family), its builders labor in vain."


If you'd like to share what's going on in your family now, we'd love to be able to pray with you!
