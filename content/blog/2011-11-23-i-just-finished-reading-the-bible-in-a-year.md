So, I just finished the One Year Bible plan through YouVersion.com! It's so exciting to have gone through God's word and seen all the different aspects of his love and plan for us!
I'm starting the plan up again tomorrow on Thanksgiving! How cool is that? It's definitely one of the most important things that I'm thankful for!


Go to [YouVersion.com](http://www.youversion.com), create an account and then go to the top of the page and select My>Reading Plans and select the Whole Bible>One Year Bible plan! You can even get the mobile app and read it on your phone (that's what I did!).


Start it tomorrow with me! It's one of the most fulfilling, crucial things you will do this coming year!
