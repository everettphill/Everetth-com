## Read it before anyone else

*AI for Christians: Biblical Wisdom for Following Jesus in the Age of Artificial Intelligence* comes out on Amazon on **Tuesday, January 26, 2027**, in ebook and paperback. Before it goes public, we're looking for 30 to 60 people to read it early and share honest feedback.

### How it works

- We send you a free ebook in mid-December.
- You read it over the next four weeks. The chapters are short, and there are fourteen of them.
- During launch week, you leave an honest review on Amazon.

Honest is the whole point. If the book helps you, say so. If it falls short, say that. Reviews from real readers are how a new book from two unknown authors gets found.

The book is yours either way, whether you review it or not, and whatever you say.

### Family, close friends, and coworkers

Amazon doesn't accept reviews from an author's family, close friends, or coworkers. If that's you, we'd still love to have you on the team. You'll read it early, tell us what you think, and help us share it on launch day. The form asks, so we can send you the right emails.

### Sign up

Signups close **Sunday, January 10**, but anyone who joins by **Sunday, December 27** gets the book on day one.
