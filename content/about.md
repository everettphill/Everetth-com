I am in the process of diving into God's goodness, grace, and glory, and this blog is a way for me to invite everyone to join me. This blog focuses on topics that can help us all identify and live out authentic Christianity. I often discuss topics like grace, salvation, joy, and studying the Bible. I publish new blog posts weekly — subscribe below to keep up.

I want to give thanks to my wife and best friend, Angela, who gives me great support in writing this blog and is my strong partner in spiritual growth. I also want to thank God for giving me my two children, Mia and Elijah. They are constant joy-bringers for us. I am deeply grateful for my church, [Vox Church](https://www.voxchurch.org), for being such a vibrant community where we have flourished.

Please read through my posts and comment on them. I would love to discuss God's truths together. If you'd like to get in touch, [contact me here](/contact/).
