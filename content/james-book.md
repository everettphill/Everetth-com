## Have you ever tried to identify what following Jesus really means?

What's at the bedrock of Christianity — beneath every Sunday morning, worship song, Bible reading, community group, and prayer? Where exactly do you find Jesus in your everyday life?

James, one of the early church leaders, penned a persuasive letter to the first followers of Jesus, outlining the joy, challenges, pain, and excitement that are part of every Christian's life. His message is bursting with counsel, warnings, and encouragement designed to give us hope and endurance as we continue on the most fulfilling adventure.

This book is about breaking down the Letter of James into the foundational elements of an authentic Christian lifestyle and applying them directly to your life. It's about taking an honest look at yourself and asking, *"Am I living like Jesus?"* It's about running toward God and asking him for help every step of the way.

**This book is about you.**
