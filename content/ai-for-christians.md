## Everyone has the chainsaw now. Most of us haven't read the manual.

Artificial intelligence is sitting in the pocket of anyone with a smartphone. It writes our emails, answers our questions about God, keeps lonely people company at two in the morning, and quietly reshapes how we think, pray, and relate to one another. Most Christians fall into one of two ditches: fearful withdrawal or uncritical embrace. Neither one is faithful.

*AI for Christians: Biblical Wisdom for Following Jesus in the Age of Artificial Intelligence* is a short, practical book for believers who want to use the most powerful tool of our time without being used by it.

In fourteen short chapters, we walk through what AI actually is, what it is already doing to our attention, our thinking, our worship, and our relationships, and how the gospel equips us to use it well. The answer isn't a better set of rules. The gospel "reorders the heart so that the tool can finally find its proper place."

**You do not need a better app. You need a better heart.**

### What's Inside

- Why AI amplifies whatever is already in the human heart, and why better rules alone cannot fix that
- The **two ditches** Christians keep falling into, and what **faithful presence** looks like instead
- How to treat AI like an **overeager intern** and stay in the **editor's chair**
- An honest look at AI companions and the surrogate relationships drawing in teens and adults alike
- **Seven guardrails** that keep AI from replacing communion with God or real human presence
- Practical wisdom for Bible study, work, parenting, and ministry

Every chapter ends with *Reflect, Pray, and Act* questions. The book also includes an **eight-session small group guide**, a **Discernment Toolbox** of scripture-anchored questions, and **A Creed for the Wise and Faithful Use of Artificial Intelligence** in versions for adults and for children.

### Written For

### The Authors

**Everett Hill** spent over a decade at Sikorsky Aircraft before becoming a pastor. He is now Operations Pastor at Vox Church in New England. He has been a heavy user of AI tools since ChatGPT launched in 2022, and he cares about what they do to how we think, act, and relate to one another. He also wrote [*James*](/james-book/).

**David Taylor** has served in ministry for over twenty-five years, including twelve years planting and pastoring a church in Turkey. He is the discipleship pastor and global missions director at Vox Church. He comes to AI asking what Jesus has to say to the world it is making.

The book grew out of a class we taught together at Vox in January 2026.
